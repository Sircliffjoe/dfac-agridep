Key Enterprises

End-to-End Value Chain Specialization

DFAC specializes in high-value aquaculture alongside resilient livestock and staple crop value chains suited for the ecology of the Niger Delta.

🐟

African Catfish (Clarias)

Flagship Aquaculture

Biometric pond monitoring from fingerlings to 1kg+ table size. Tracking water temperature, pH, feed conversion ratios (FCR), and mortality rate controls.

Cycle: ~150 Days Benchmark FCR: 1.25
🐠

Nile Tilapia

Freshwater Aquaculture

Controlled hatchery fingerling distribution, earthen and concrete pond management, and high-protein feed optimization tailored for regional freshwater streams.

Cycle: ~180 Days Benchmark FCR: 1.40
🐌

Heliculture (Snail Farming)

Specialty Livestock

High-value export and hospitality supply. Structured pen construction, climate-humidity monitoring, and organic calcium-fortified feed programs.

Cycle: 8-12 Months Market: Premium/Export
🌱

Cassava & Arable Crops

Staple Food Security

Disease-resistant stem cutting propagation, soil preparation tracking, mechanized harvesting forecasts, and industrial starch off-taker linkage.

Anchored in the Niger Delta

Agricultural production in riverine and coastal environments requires deep local context. DFAC operates field clusters across Delta, Bayelsa, and Rivers states, accommodating low digital literacy, irregular power, and remote water access.

📍 Delta: Warri & Ughelli 📍 Bayelsa: Yenagoa & Ogbia 📍 Rivers: Port Harcourt & Obio-Akpor

Why Decision Automation Matters

Data stored in a spreadsheet is too late to save a crop or pond. AgriDEP’s automated rules engine actively evaluates biometric logs:

  • 14-Day Off-take Match: Automatically alerts verified buyers when ponds approach table-size maturity.
  • Early Warning System: Triggers prioritized field visits when growth falls behind baseline curves.
  • Input Gate Assurance: Locks subsequent credit disbursements until verified supplier delivery is confirmed.
