Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) Alignment

Smallholder data is personal and sensitive. DFAC AgriDEP is engineered around privacy-by-design principles:

  • Explicit Consent Capture: Every registered producer signs verifiable consent defining exactly which external parties (lenders, buyers) may view their information.
  • Field-Level Encryption: National Identification Numbers (NIN) and telephone numbers are encrypted at rest using industry-standard symmetric cryptography.
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Every query or access to financial ledgers or personal smallholder data is permanently recorded in our system audit trail.
  • Buyer Privacy Shield: Commercial off-takers see aggregated regional volumes; farmer identities remain anonymized until bilateral supply contracts are confirmed.

Aligned with International Development Frameworks

Designed to satisfy rigorous Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) reporting obligations for agricultural donor agencies, including the Foundation for Partnership Initiatives in the Niger Delta (PIND) and GIZ.
