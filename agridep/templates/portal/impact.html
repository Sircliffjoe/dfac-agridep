Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) Alignment

Smallholder farmers in the Niger Delta deserve the exact same institutional privacy standards as corporate enterprises. DFAC AgriDEP incorporates privacy-by-design at every architecture layer:

1. Explicit Consent Signatures

Producers sign granular consent specifying which lenders or off-takers may access their yield profiles.

2. Field-Level Encryption

NIN national identity numbers, BVNs, and telephone contacts are AES-256 encrypted at rest.

3. Cryptographic Audit Trails

Every query into smallholder financial ledgers is permanently recorded with user identity, timestamp, and IP.

4. Buyer Privacy Shield

Commercial buyers view aggregated cluster volumes; individual farmer identities are masked until contracts confirm.
