AQUACULTURE VALUE CHAIN

African Catfish (Clarias gariepinus) & Tilapia

Aquaculture represents the economic lifeblood of Niger Delta communities. DFAC AgriDEP manages the complete farming cycle: from certified hatchery fingerling procurement, water parameters (dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature, and ammonia control), to high-efficiency feed conversion and sorting into table-size Grade A harvests.

150 Days

1.0 – 1.2 kg
