About Doctor Fish Agricultural Consult

Pioneering Sustainable Agriculture in the Niger Delta

Doctor Fish Agricultural Consult (DFAC) sits at the nexus of smallholder empowerment, commercial agricultural finance, and modern supply-chain logistics.

🎯

Our Mission

To eliminate information asymmetry and post-harvest losses across Niger Delta smallholder ecosystems by combining grassroots agricultural extension with real-time biometric data and direct market linkages.

👁️

Our Vision

A thriving, de-risked agricultural economy in southern Nigeria where smallholders achieve predictable livelihoods, buyers obtain reliable premium food supply, and financial institutions invest with total confidence.

The Challenge We Solve

Smallholder agriculture in the Niger Delta has historically been held back by disconnected records: paper attendance sheets for training, standalone loan ledgers kept by financial institutions, handwritten farm visit logs, and informal last-minute price negotiations with buyers.

When each stakeholder maintains their own isolated version of the truth, decisions slow down, reporting to development partners becomes arduous, and biological risk is discovered only after fish or crops have been lost.

DFAC AgriDEP replaces this fragmentation with a single source of truth anchored on the Farmer, the Farm, and the Buyer. When an extension agent records a pond inspection in Delta State, that update simultaneously calibrates harvest forecasts for waiting processors and reassures the agricultural lender monitoring the loan tranche.
