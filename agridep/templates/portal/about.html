About DFAC AgriDEP

Pioneering Sustainable Agribusiness in the Niger Delta

Doctor Fish Agricultural Consult (DFAC) bridges grassroots smallholder empowerment with modern fintech underwriting, commercial off-taker contracts, and institutional donor governance.

Our Mission

To eliminate market information asymmetry, predatory middlemen, and post-harvest biological losses across the Niger Delta by equipping smallholders with real-time agronomic data, transparent credit scoring, and guaranteed off-take contracts.

Our Vision

A self-sustaining, de-risked agricultural economy across southern Nigeria where smallholder farmers achieve predictable incomes, food processors source uninterrupted high-grade produce, and financial institutions invest with verifiable confidence.

Core Value Proposition

Replacing Fragmentation with a Unified Single Truth

Smallholder agriculture across Delta, Bayelsa, and Rivers State has historically suffered from disconnected data silos: paper attendance sheets stored in filing cabinets, stand-alone bank loan spreadsheets, handwritten field inspection reports, and volatile spot-market pricing.

When every actor works in isolation, biological risks such as water pollution, disease outbreaks, or feed shortages are discovered too late—after entire ponds have suffered mortality or crop yields have collapsed.

DFAC AgriDEP connects all stakeholders to a single unified digital ledger. When a field officer records pond biometrics or water pH in Ughelli, that single data point recalibrates the farmer's harvest date for commercial buyers in Warri and updates the lender's expected recovery value in real time.

Offline-First Accessibility

Designed for muddy pond embankments and remote farming communities with zero cell towers. Field agents sync seamlessly offline; smallholders interact via USSD (*384*40#) and SMS.

Data Privacy & NDPA 2023

Smallholder personal data, NIN credentials, and bank accounts are strictly protected under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) with cryptographic role-based masking.

Donor M&E Accountability

Built-in automated indicators for PIND Foundation, GIZ, and BOA reporting, delivering instant Excel and PDF donor monitoring sheets with tamper-proof audit trails.
